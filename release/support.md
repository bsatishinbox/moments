# Moment support

Moment displays the days, hours, or minutes remaining until your chosen age milestone.

- Open Settings to set your age and milestone. The default is age 37 with a milestone of 60.
- Select 70 or enter another age to update the milestone. Your saved age anchor is preserved when only the milestone changes.
- Use your birthday for an exact calendar date. Age-only setup assumes you just turned the age you enter.
- Tap Days, Hours, or Minutes to change the main counter.
- iPhone and a paired Apple Watch synchronize when WatchConnectivity is available. Open both apps if a change has not arrived yet.
- Reaching a milestone displays zero; choose a new milestone in Settings.

To report a problem, open an issue at https://github.com/bsatishinbox/moments/issues with your device model, operating-system version, and steps to reproduce it. Issues are public; omit your birthday and other personal information.

Read the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) for details about locally saved settings.
