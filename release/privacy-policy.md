# Moment privacy policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Moment is a personal countdown to an age milestone you choose. It does not predict lifespan.

## Information stored by the app

Moment saves your approximate age or optional birthday, target age and date, and preferred display unit in preferences on your device. You can change these values in Settings. The app does not require an account.

The iPhone and Apple Watch apps exchange these settings through Apple's WatchConnectivity service when the devices are paired and communication is available. Android and the web preview do not synchronize with Apple devices or with each other.

## Collection and sharing

Moment has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated server. The app does not send your settings to the developer, advertisers, or analytics providers.

Apple's operating system may include app preferences in device backups according to your device settings. The Android app disables operating-system backup. The web preview stores its settings in the browser's local storage; its website host may process ordinary requests needed to serve the site under that host's policies.

## Managing your information

Edit your timeline in the app's settings. Removing the native app removes its local preferences, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behavior. To remove saved Apple companion settings, remove the app from both the iPhone and Apple Watch. To remove web-preview settings, clear site data in your browser.

## Questions

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/bsatishinbox/moments/issues. GitHub issues are public; do not include your birthday or other personal information. GitHub processes submissions under its own privacy policy.

Any change to the app's data behavior will be reflected in an updated policy.
