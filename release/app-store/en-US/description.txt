See your time in perspective.

Moment is a simple countdown to a personal age milestone. Enter your age, choose the age you want to count toward, and see the time ahead in total days, hours, or minutes.

• Choose your own target age and change it whenever you like.
• Add your birthday for an exact calendar countdown.
• Switch between days, hours, and minutes with a tap.
• Keep your timeline on your device, with no account required.
• Check your countdown on Apple Watch, with settings that sync with your paired iPhone when available.

Moment uses the dates you choose. It does not predict your lifespan or provide medical advice.
